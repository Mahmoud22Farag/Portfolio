Method

How I work

The same four steps for every client, so results can be checked against what was promised.

Plan

Objective, market, personas, offer, budget and KPI targets, written before launch.

Test

15 to 20 days: audiences, creatives and channels (WhatsApp vs lead form) against each other.

Report

Ad test report: plan vs result, lead-sheet quality, what to stop and what to keep.

Scale

Budget moves weekly to the winning creatives and audiences; new tests run in parallel.
